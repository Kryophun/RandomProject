How to play

Eat the fruit to grow. Avoid your own tail. In Walls mode, avoid the board edges; in Wrap mode, crossing an edge continues from the opposite side. Use the arrow keys, WASD, direction buttons, or a swipe across the board to turn. Press Space to pause. Campaign mode adds moving enemies and clustered stone walls. Eat the rainbow apple to become temporarily invincible against enemies and defeat them on contact. Walls and the snake's own body remain dangerous. Every fifth Campaign level is a boss battle: eat apples for ammunition, then press F, Enter, or the Spit apple button to hit the boss three times.
